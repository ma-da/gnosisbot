Account security

Your Gnosis identity is controlled by your Ed25519 private account key. The site uses your public key and revocable browser sessions; it does not retain a backup copy of your private key.

No account recovery: If your private key is lost, Gnosis currently cannot recover your account or its account-bound credits and history. Keep secure, independent backups.

Never send your private key, key file, seed phrase or wallet recovery phrase to support staff, moderators, other users or automated agents. Anyone who obtains a private credential may gain control of the corresponding account or wallet.

Solana wallet registration, when available, will verify control using an appropriate signed challenge, not by requesting secret keys. Connecting a wallet to Gnosis never requires sharing its recovery phrase.

Only follow links and payment instructions from the official Gnosis Bot website. Report suspicious messages and impersonation attempts through the site's official contact channel.
