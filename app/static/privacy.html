GNOSIS.BOT / SITE POLICIES

Privacy Policy

Gnosis.bot · Effective November 21, 2026

In short: Your questions are not made public by default. We keep account-backed readings private to the account holder, publish aggregate research statistics, and offer a separate, unchecked choice to let us consider your question for public research. Gnosis.bot is an independent experimental project. Contact us through the website contact form for privacy questions or requests.

What information we handle

  • Website visits: Our hosting and security services may process IP addresses, device/browser information, request times, and similar technical logs needed to deliver and protect the site.
  • Accounts: We store your public cryptographic key or account identifier, login/session records, credit balances and transactions, and related security information. Your private key is generated and kept under your control; it is not sent to us for account authentication.
  • Readings: For account-backed I Ching and Rune consultations, we store your optional question, inquiry style, reading results, timestamps, provider and verification records, and whether you chose public-research consent. Reading history is available to the account holder after authentication. The I Ching Classic/System Random option normally runs in your browser without saving a reading to your account. If you check the research option for Classic, the question and associated casting data are sent to a restricted research record.
  • Email and contact: If you choose to verify an email for notifications, we store it encrypted and use it for account-related messages. If you send a message through the contact form, we receive the name, email, and message you provide. Contact messages are stored encrypted. First-party abuse prevention uses expiring form tokens, email-based submission limits and local content rules; it does not use IP-based limits or message fingerprints. Suspicious messages may be quarantined and are eligible for removal after 14 days by routine contact activity or administrator maintenance.

What we use it for

We use this information to deliver and verify readings, maintain accounts and credits, prevent abuse, send requested notifications, troubleshoot problems, meet legal obligations, and study aggregate patterns in reading results. We do not sell personal information or use submitted questions for targeted advertising.

Public research and transparency

The public Research Observatory reports aggregate results—such as hexagram frequencies and changing-line statistics—not individual questions or account identifiers. These aggregate statistics can include account-backed readings regardless of the question-publication checkbox.

Selecting “Use my question for public research” gives permission to consider that question for a public research report or dataset. It does not publish anything automatically. We review material before public release and aim to remove identifying details, but free-text questions can still reveal private information; please do not include names or sensitive details. Without that opt-in, your question is not authorized for public research publication.

The separate public quantum-transparency log may include limited non-identifying technical details, timestamps, and cryptographic commitments used to audit readings. It does not publicly expose your question, email, account identifier, or private reading receipt. Signed public records and aggregated statistics may be difficult or impossible to retract after publication.

Who helps operate the service

We use third-party infrastructure and services to run the site. These can include DigitalOcean (hosting), Cloudflare (web delivery/security), and Resend (optional notification emails). External randomness or hardware providers, such as QCI, NIST, and IBM Quantum, may receive technical requests needed for their services; we do not intentionally send your private question as part of a randomness request. Public frontend fonts and JavaScript libraries are served locally. We may also share data when legally required or to protect the service.

Cookies and security

We use a necessary login session cookie for signed-in accounts; it can last up to 90 days unless you sign out or it is revoked. Browser-side features may use local storage or memory to maintain a reading or recover a pending request. We do not intentionally set advertising-tracking cookies. We use HTTPS, access controls, and safeguards for stored information, but no internet service can guarantee perfect security. Keep your private key and any downloaded account files secure.

Keeping or deleting data

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to operate accounts and readings, protect against abuse, maintain audit and research integrity, and meet legal obligations. We do not currently promise an automatic deletion date for saved readings or audit records. Some signed verification records, backups, or de-identified aggregate data may be retained for longer.

You can contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent for future use of an unpublished question. We may need to verify that you control the relevant account, and some records may need to be retained for security, accounting, legal, or technical integrity. We will consider requests and honor applicable privacy-law rights; information already made public may not be fully retractable.

Children, changes, and contact

The service is not designed for children under 13. Please do not submit personal information about a child. We may update this policy as the site develops and will revise the date above when we do.

For privacy requests or questions, use the Gnosis.bot contact form. Please do not submit your private key, account secret, or unnecessary sensitive information in a contact message.
