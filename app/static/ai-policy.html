AI Policy

Gnosis.bot · Effective November 21, 2026

What AI does—and does not do

Gnosis.bot uses artificial intelligence to help translate, research, organize, and interpret historical material. AI-generated writing is separate from the process that produces a reading's random or quantum measurements. AI does not make the coins, qubits, or runes come out a particular way. Nor does an AI interpretation establish that a reading predicts the future or that consciousness affects quantum measurements.

Where the I Ching text comes from

Our Gnosis Edition of the Book of Changes starts with classical Chinese materials, not with AI inventions presented as ancient sayings:

  • Classical foundation: the received Zhouyi (周易), including each hexagram's Judgment, six original line statements, and the special use-nine/use-six passages for hexagrams 1 and 2; plus the Great Image (大象) from the Ten Wings. This is not a complete translation of the Ten Wings.
  • Taoist perspective: Liu Yiming (劉一明), Zhouyi Chanzhen (周易闡真), a later Taoist commentary on the Changes.
  • Confucian perspective: Cheng Yi (程頤), Yichuan Yizhuan (伊川易傳), a later Confucian commentary.

For example, the source records link to historical Chinese editions of Zhouyi, hexagram 1, Liu Yiming's commentary, and Cheng Yi's commentary. The record for each hexagram identifies its relevant source passages.

The original Chinese passages and commentaries are the historical source material. Our English renderings, explanations, questions for reflection, and modern applications are AI-assisted translations or adaptations prepared for Gnosis.bot. They are not original writings by Liu Yiming or Cheng Yi, and should not be mistaken for direct quotations unless expressly labeled. Modern wording can simplify, combine, or interpret ideas in ways the original authors did not state.

We identify the tradition behind each interpretation. Source records preserve links to historical Chinese editions, specific source revisions, retrieval information, and checksums where available. The original 0.9.0 release manifest identifies deepseek-v4-pro as a generation model; subsequent versions may use other tools. Corpus metadata also records editorial status and correction history. Books by modern translators may be cited as further reading, not as the source of Gnosis.bot's English wording. Readers who need scholarly precision should compare the Chinese texts and independent published translations.

How the edition was prepared

The current I Ching corpus was developed through a documented source → AI translation → interpretive synthesis → checks and corrections → human review process. AI was used to draft translations and historical/contemporary commentaries; automated comparisons and correction passes were used to identify problems. Human editorial review remains a separate step. The original 0.9.0 corpus was marked AI-assisted; final human review pending. Approval of one passage should not be interpreted as independent verification of every passage.

AI can mistranslate classical Chinese, miss historical context, invent connections, or sound more certain than the sources justify. We may correct or replace interpretations as review continues. Our text is intended for reflection and learning, not as professional advice or a definitive scholarly translation.

Planned personalized AI reading interpreter

A personalized AI interpreter is planned but is not part of the current standard reading process. If you choose to use it, your question and the relevant reading information (such as hexagrams, moving lines, or rune results and interpretive text) will be sent to an AI processing service to generate a response. If an external model provider is used, it will receive the information needed for that request. That is different from using a stored interpretation or generating randomness.

Before you use an AI interpreter, we will identify the feature as AI-powered, explain what information is sent, identify the processing provider and link to its privacy terms when applicable, and show any applicable credit cost. You will choose whether to make that AI request. If you do not use the feature, your question will not be sent to an AI interpreter merely because you received a reading.

The “Use my question for public research” checkbox is a separate, optional permission. It does not authorize an AI interpreter request, and choosing AI interpretation will not by itself authorize public publication of your question. Neither permission, by itself, authorizes Gnosis.bot to train models on your private questions. Any proposed training use would require a separate, explicit choice. Do not include sensitive personal information in questions sent to an AI service. An external provider's processing and retention terms will matter; we will update this policy and the Privacy Policy before enabling the feature.

Transparency, feedback, and contact

We aim to distinguish historical source text, AI-assisted translation, contemporary adaptation, and personalized AI output wherever they appear. We will not present AI-generated text as an independently authenticated ancient source. Please report suspected translation errors or misleading attribution using our contact form.
